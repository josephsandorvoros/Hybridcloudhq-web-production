How to interpret this

Usable is the data you actually keep (daily volume × retention). Compressed applies each item's compression/dedup ratio. Raw adds redundancy — either a replica factor or the drive count a RAID level needs. Purchased adds your growth buffer on top.

GFS retention counts daily snapshots as 1 day each, monthly as 30 days, and yearly as 365 days. RAID 10 uses half the drive count for capacity; RAID 5/6 lose one/two drives to parity.

Cloud comparison prices the compressed stored data across hot/cool/archive tiers — useful for a hybrid on-prem vs. cloud conversation. Treat all outputs as a planning baseline, then refine with measured compression ratios and real drive pricing.
